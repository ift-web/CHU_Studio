About the Studio

Design rooted
in intention

Chu Studio is an independent design practice based in Taiwan, working at the intersection of visual identity, typography, and spatial thinking. We believe the most enduring design is built on restraint, clarity, and cultural resonance.

Every project begins with deep listening — understanding not just what a brand looks like, but what it stands for and who it speaks to. The result is work that feels inevitable.

How We Work

The Process

01
Discovery
We begin with deep research into your brand, audience, and competitive landscape. This phase establishes the strategic foundation everything else is built upon.
02
Concept
Multiple visual directions are explored, each grounded in a clear rationale. We present ideas that challenge and expand — not just execute the obvious.
03
Refinement
The chosen direction is developed with meticulous care. Typography, spacing, color — every variable is tested until the work is exactly right.
04
Delivery
Final files are delivered with comprehensive guidelines. We ensure you have everything needed to maintain the integrity of the work across all applications.
